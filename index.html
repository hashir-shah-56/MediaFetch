GOOD TO KNOW

A little more clarity.

The essentials about MediaFetch
and where we're headed.

MediaFetch analyzes Instagram and YouTube media links through configured providers. YouTube metadata and authorized-content video downloads use separate services.

Instagram post, reel and video URLs, plus YouTube video and Shorts URLs, are recognized. Profiles, stories, channels and playlist-only pages are not supported. Recognition does not confirm media existence or access.

The backend temporarily stores files while preparing authorized downloads and removes them after streaming or failure. Startup cleanup handles stale jobs left by a crash. URLs and identifiers are sent to the configured services only as needed; ordinary host/provider logs may still apply.

No. Private Instagram content is not supported. MediaFetch does not bypass sign-in requirements, privacy settings, or platform access controls.

Each platform has different capabilities and access rules. Instagram actions depend on the provider response. YouTube offers metadata and a separate authorized MP4 workflow; a recognized URL does not guarantee a downloadable format.

This frontend preview is free to use and requires no account. Pricing for any future service has not been decided.
